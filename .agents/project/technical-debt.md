## Rules

- Every shortcut that affects correctness, security, performance, operability, or maintainability should have an entry.
- Describe observable impact and a concrete trigger, such as traffic, data volume, incident rate, or a planned migration.
- Do not turn normal future possibilities into debt without evidence or a committed trade-off.
